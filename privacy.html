Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

This is an English translation provided for convenience. The German version is legally binding.

SHAPEVO is an app for tracking strength training and body progress, developed according to the privacy-first principle: all training and body data as well as photos remain exclusively stored locally on your device. There is no server of our own, no tracking and no analytics software. This policy describes in detail which data is processed and when.

1.Controller

The controller for SHAPEVO within the meaning of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is the developer of the app. Contact for privacy questions: schmitz873@gmail.com.

2.Profile, training and body data

When you first start the app, it asks for your name and gender. The name is used only to address you personally, the gender only to select matching exercise illustrations. Both are stored exclusively locally on your device and can be changed at any time in the settings.

Exercises, training sets, repetitions, weight and circumference measurements are stored in a local SQLite database on your device. This data is never automatically transmitted to the developer, to advertising networks or to any other third parties. The developer has no access to this data.

3.Photos & camera/photo library access

You can optionally add progress photos to your body measurements. For this, the app asks for permission to access the camera or photo library the first time. Photos you take or select are cropped, compressed and stored exclusively in the app's private storage area on your device – not in your general system gallery and not on a server of the developer.

4.Export and import (data backup)

You can export your database and photos as a single backup file (ZIP), for example to transfer them to a new device. This feature is triggered only by you.

  • When exporting, the app opens your operating system's share sheet. Where the file is saved or sent – e.g. to the Files app, to cloud storage such as iCloud Drive or Google Drive, via AirDrop or by email – is decided by you alone.
  • SHAPEVO itself does not connect to any cloud service and does not transmit any data to the developer or to third parties. Processing by the service you choose is subject to that service's privacy policy.
  • The backup file is not encrypted and contains your profile, training and body data as well as photos. Please store it carefully.
  • When importing, you select a backup file using your device's file picker. Its contents replace the local data in the app.

5.No tracking, no analytics, no advertising

SHAPEVO contains no analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs. No usage, device or location data is collected or transmitted to third parties.

6.Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app. Exported backup files remain wherever you saved them until you delete them yourself.

7.Your rights

Since all data is stored exclusively on your device or in backup files you saved yourself and the developer has no access to it whatsoever, you can exercise your rights of access, rectification and erasure directly yourself – for example by editing or deleting entries in the app or by deleting your backup files. If you have any questions, you can contact us at any time at the address above.

Regardless of this, you have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the authority responsible for your place of residence.

8.Changes to this policy

This privacy policy will be updated as necessary if the app's functionality or legal requirements change. The version with the date stated above applies.
