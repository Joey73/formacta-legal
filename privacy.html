Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 September 2026

This is an English translation provided for convenience. The German version is legally binding.

FormActa is an app for tracking strength training and body progress, developed according to the privacy-first principle: all training and body data as well as photos remain exclusively stored locally on your device. There is no server of our own, no tracking and no analytics software. This policy describes in detail which data is processed and when.

1.Controller

The controller for FormActa within the meaning of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is the developer of the app. Contact for privacy questions: schmitz873@gmail.com.

2.Training and body data

Exercises, training sets, repetitions, weight and circumference measurements are stored in a local SQLite database on your device. This data is never automatically transmitted to the developer, to advertising networks or to any other third parties. The developer has no access to this data.

3.Photos & camera/photo library access

You can optionally add progress photos to your body measurements. For this, the app asks for permission to access the camera or photo library the first time. Photos you take or select are cropped, compressed and stored exclusively in the app's private storage area on your device – not in your general system gallery and not on a server of the developer.

4.Optional cloud backup via Google Drive

If you wish, you can back up your database and photos to your own Google Drive account. This feature is inactive by default and is triggered only by you.

  • Sign-in uses OAuth 2.0 with PKCE directly between your device and Google. Neither FormActa nor the developer ever receives your Google password.
  • Only the drive.file permission scope is requested. This means the app can only access the backup file it creates in your Drive itself – not any other files or folders in your Google Drive.
  • The backup file is stored in your own Google Drive account. The developer has no access to it and does not operate a server through which the data passes.
  • Export and import work as a simple “back up everything” / “restore everything” – there is no ongoing automatic background sync.
  • You can revoke access at any time in your Google account under “Third-party apps with account access” and delete the backup file directly in Google Drive.

5.No tracking, no analytics, no advertising

FormActa contains no analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs. No usage, device or location data is collected or transmitted to third parties.

6.Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app. A Google Drive backup remains until you delete it yourself in Google Drive or replace it with a new backup.

7.Your rights

Since all data is stored exclusively on your device or in your own Google account and the developer has no access to it whatsoever, you can exercise your rights of access, rectification and erasure directly yourself – for example by editing or deleting entries in the app or by deleting the backup file in Google Drive. If you have any questions, you can contact us at any time at the address above.

Regardless of this, you have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the authority responsible for your place of residence.

8.Changes to this policy

This privacy policy will be updated as necessary if the app's functionality or legal requirements change. The version with the date stated above applies.
